---
title: "Workspace subdomain"
description: "Find your workspace subdomain and use it to connect an assistant."
---

Every NeetoRecord workspace has its own subdomain. It is the part of your workspace URL before `neetorecord.com`. For `https://spinkart.neetorecord.com`, the subdomain is `spinkart`.

The MCP server URL is the same for every workspace, `https://connect.neetorecord.com/mcp/messages`. The subdomain is how you tell NeetoRecord which workspace the assistant can reach.

## Find your subdomain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Sign in to NeetoRecord in your browser and look at the address bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the part before neetorecord.com">
    For `https://green-earth.neetorecord.com`, the subdomain is `green-earth`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where you use it

- **OAuth.** The first sign-in screen asks you to connect your workspace. Enter only the subdomain, such as `green-earth`, not the full URL or an email address.
- **API key.** Create the key in the workspace you want the assistant to reach. One key reaches one workspace.

See [Authentication](/mcp/authentication) for both routes.
